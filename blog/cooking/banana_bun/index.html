[comment]: (Making Bread (6): Banana Bun) [comment]: <brief> (Making banana bun using a youtube recipe.)</p> <h1>Making Bread (6): Banana Bun</h1> <h2>Ingredients</h2> <table> <thead> <tr> <th style="text-align:center">Ingredients</th> <th style="text-align:center">Amount</th> </tr> </thead> <tbody> <tr> <td style="text-align:center">Milk</td> <td style="text-align:center">64 g</td> </tr> <tr> <td style="text-align:center">Yeast</td> <td style="text-align:center">3.5 g</td> </tr> <tr> <td style="text-align:center">Bread Flour</td> <td style="text-align:center">250 g</td> </tr> <tr> <td style="text-align:center">Salt</td> <td style="text-align:center">3.5 g</td> </tr> <tr> <td style="text-align:center">Banana</td> <td style="text-align:center">147 g</td> </tr> <tr> <td style="text-align:center">Butter</td> <td style="text-align:center">27 g</td> </tr> </tbody> </table> <h2>Steps</h2> <ol> <li>Mix yeast into room temperature milk, activate for 10 minutes</li> <li>Mash the banana</li> <li>Slowly mix the flour with the banana puree, and mix the yeast mixture gradually</li> <li>Mix the dough evenly until it's slightly sticky</li> <li>Add the soften butter to the dough and mix it until the butter is fully absorbed</li> <li>Let the dough rise in a greezed bowl for roughly 1 hour</li> <li>Flatten the dough to deflate</li> <li>Divide the dough into 9 pieces, flatten and deflate again</li> <li>Shape them and put them into the baking pan. Proof for 40 to 45 minutes</li> <li>Brush a thin layer of milk, bake for 20 minutes in 180C</li> <li>Brush a thin layer of melted butter</li> </ol> <h2>Images</h2> <h3>v1.0</h3> <table> <thead> <tr> <th>before</th> <th>after</th> </tr> </thead> <tbody> <tr> <td><img src="/images/blog/banana_bun/before.jpg" alt="before_img"></td> <td><img src="/images/blog/banana_bun/after.jpg" alt="after_img"></td> </tr> </tbody> </table> <h2>Reference</h2> <ul> <li>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkKhxj2lt_Y</li> </ul>
