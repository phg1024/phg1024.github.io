About

Peihong is currently a Senior Staff Research Engineer at Meta Reality Labs.

Before joining Meta, he completed a PhD in Computer Science at Texas A&M University. He received a B.S. in Physics from Peking University in 2010.

The work collected here moves between machine learning, graphics and drawing — renderers and models, embedded gadgets and crayon picture books — with an emphasis on clean presentation and durable ideas.
