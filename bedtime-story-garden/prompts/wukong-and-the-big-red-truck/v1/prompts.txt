Wukong and the Big Red Truck - Production notes and illustration prompts

Mode: built-in image generation. References: approved Wukong crayon-doodle cast and this book’s cover. Story text: Chalkboard SE Regular, 36 points. 16 pages including the cover. Original selected assets are archived in the character library and project work folder.

Fire-safety references (adult production notes, not story narration):
US Fire Administration, Fire Safety for Children: https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/at-risk-audiences/children/
USFA/Sesame Street educator guide: https://www.usfa.fema.gov/downloads/pdf/publications/ss_educators_guide.pdf
NFPA Sparky, Firetrucks: https://sparky.org/firetrucks
The book depicts a supervised station visit. Its basic alarm/escape message is to get outside and stay outside; it is not a complete home escape plan.

COVER for Wukong and the Big Red Truck. Top 43% entirely blank. Low three-quarter scene of a BIG friendly red FIRE ENGINE parked outside a brick fire station, silver side compartments, one coiled yellow fire hose, folded aluminum ladder on roof, two black wheels visible on this side, red/blue warning lights unlit. Truck faces LEFT, angled large across background lower half. In foreground Wukong without helmet gazes up in amazement, one hand at cheek, other at his side, tail curled up. Beside him FIREFIGHTER BEN is a warm adult male human with medium warm brown skin, short black hair, short rounded black mustache, kind dark eyes and broad nose; bright YELLOW firefighter helmet, tan turnout coat and pants with yellow-silver reflective bands, black boots. Ben crouches to child's eye level, pointing gently to the truck with one hand, other hand rests at knee. Pigsy peeks from behind Wukong, smiling. Cozy crayon sun and two little doodle sparkles, no emergency, no fire, no smoke. Strong inviting cover composition, clearly a visit to learn. Keep all characters below top43%.
