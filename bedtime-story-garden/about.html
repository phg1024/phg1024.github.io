About the stories

Stories to share at bedtime.

A growing collection of English bedtime picture books maintained by Peihong Guo. Wukong, Pigsy and their friends explore everyday life through curiosity, practice and kindness.

Free, with optional kindness.

Every published story is free to read, download and print for noncommercial home and classroom storytime. Support is entirely optional and never affects access. Text and illustrations are AI-assisted, with human direction and editing.

No additional commercial reuse license is granted. Traditional characters are child-friendly interpretations for this series, unaffiliated with film or television adaptations. The published PDFs are the complete public edition.

A small signature.

Published PDFs carry a faint Wukong & Friends · Peihong Guo attribution. An Ed25519-signed edition manifest also lets you check that a downloaded file matches the publication. This is not a certificate-based PDF signature or third-party identity verification.
