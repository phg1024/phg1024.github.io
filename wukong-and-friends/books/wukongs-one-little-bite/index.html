Sun Wukong’s One Little Bite

One tiny taste leads Wukong toward a warm, friendly supper.

The whole story, in words

The books are printed in English. The text is here so it can be read on any screen, searched, and copied out loud.

The sun sank low.
Sun Wukong hugged his peach.
“Only peaches for me!”

“Come help with supper,”
said Pigsy.
Wukong washed a carrot.
Splash! His nose got wet.

Sandy stirred a little pot.
A leaf fell onto
Wukong’s peach.
“My peach has a hat!”
He gave a little giggle.

A sweet smell filled the air.
“What is that?”
asked Wukong.
“A warm carrot,” said Sandy.

Wukong took one tiny bite.
“Oh! Sweet and soft.”
He set his peach
beside his bowl.

“How about a little green?”
Wukong tried a small leaf.
“Hmm… soft and juicy!”

He tried rice
and little tofu cubes.
A little of this.
A little of that.
“My bowl has new friends!”

The moon shone over supper.
Wukong ate one last
peach bite.
“I like more than peaches.”

Wukong snuggled into bed.
“Goodnight, little bowl.
Goodnight, friends.”
Tomorrow, he could try
something new.

How do I print a little book?

Download the booklet edition. Print on US Letter, landscape, double-sided with short-edge flipping, at actual size / 100%, one PDF page per sheet, in normal page order. Each PDF page already contains two book pages.

This book uses 3 sheets, with 2 blank book pages added at the end. Nest the sheets, fold through the center and staple along the fold.

Editions and print files

AI-assisted creation with human direction and editing. Each edition keeps its own text and illustrations. Reading and booklet PDFs carry a subtle attribution watermark.
