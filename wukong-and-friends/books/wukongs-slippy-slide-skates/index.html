Wukong’s Slippy-Slide Skates

His feet feel like tiny sleds. A little tumble, then one more try!

The whole story, in words

The books are printed in English. The text is here so it can be read on any screen, searched, and copied out loud.

Wukong watched them glide.
“Look! Flying feet!”
“I want to try!”

He put on his skates.
Clomp. Clomp.
“My feet have tiny sleds!”

Helmet on. Mittens on.
Coach Otter checked
his skates.
“Ready for little steps?”

Wukong stepped onto the ice.
His feet slid apart.
“Wait for me, feet!”

Whoops! Down he went.
His bottom met the ice.
“Hello, chilly floor.”

“Learning takes practice,”
said Coach Otter.
“Let’s get up together.”

Little steps. Bent knees.
“Like a penguin!” said Rabbit.
Wukong waddled and giggled.

Push… glide.
Push… glide.
One tiny slide!
Then another.

Then came another tumble.
“I thought I had it.”
He sat beside Pigsy.
“Rest with me,” said Pigsy.

They warmed their paws.
They watched
the swirling snow.
“I’ll try again,” said Wukong.

He came back to practice.
A little step. A little glide.
Wobbles and all.

Then his feet found a rhythm.
Push… glide.
Push… glide.
“Look! I’m doing it!”

They made a gentle circle.
Rabbit led. Pigsy followed.
Wukong glided along.
Six happy skates!

The rink lights twinkled.
“See you soon, ice!”
His feet were tired.
His smile was not.

Wukong curled under his quilt.
In his dreams,
he glided past the stars.
Slowly. Softly.
All the way to sleep.

How do I print a little book?

Download the booklet edition. Print on US Letter, landscape, double-sided with short-edge flipping, at actual size / 100%, one PDF page per sheet, in normal page order. Each PDF page already contains two book pages.

This book uses 4 sheets, with 0 blank book pages added at the end. Nest the sheets, fold through the center and staple along the fold.

Editions and print files
