Sun Wukong’s Noisy Nap

Wukong wants a nap, but his friends’ helpful ideas are a little noisy.

English bedtime picture book · About age 4 · Free to read and print

The whole story, in words

The books are printed in English. The text is here so it can be read on any screen, searched, and copied out loud.

Sun Wukong yawned.
“Time for a nap!”
He found a shady peach tree.

Pigsy brought a pillow.
“Soft as a cloud!”
Then his tummy went
RUMBLE.

“A snack first,” said Wukong.
Munch, munch.
Pigsy’s tummy grew quiet.

Sandy waved a leaf fan.
A leaf tickled Wukong’s nose.
“Ah… ah… ACHOO!”

A little bird sang a song.
“TWEET! TWEET!”
Wukong sat up.
“A little softer, please!”

Pigsy chewed softly.
Sandy fanned slowly.
The bird sang, “Twee…
twee…”
Wukong smiled.

Wukong blew one tiny puff.
A little cloud curled around
his feet.
“Just right,” he whispered.

Soon, his friends were sleepy
too.
Pigsy snored. “Snrrr…”
Wukong gave one last giggle.

Under the peach tree,
everyone slept.
The leaves went hush.
The clouds drifted by.
Even heroes need a nap.

How do I print a little book?

Download the booklet edition. Print on US Letter, landscape, double-sided with short-edge flipping, at actual size / 100%, one PDF page per sheet, in normal page order. Each PDF page already contains two book pages.

This book uses 3 sheets, with 2 blank book pages added at the end. Nest the sheets, fold through the center and staple along the fold.

Editions and print files
