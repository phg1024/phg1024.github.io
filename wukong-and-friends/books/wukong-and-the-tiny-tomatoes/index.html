Wukong and the Tiny Tomatoes

Wukong and Ao Bing plant tiny tomatoes, water them, and wait. A little rain magic helps; patience brings a sweet harvest to share.

English bedtime picture book · About age 4 · Free to read and print

The whole story, in words

The books are printed in English. The text is here so it can be read on any screen, searched, and copied out loud.

By his front door,
Wukong had a sunny spot.
“Let’s grow tiny tomatoes!”

Ao Bing brought two plants.
They dug two little holes.
“One for you. One for me!”

In went the plants.
Pat, pat went the soil.
Wukong patted his tummy, too.

Wukong tipped his little can.
“A drink for you...”
Oops! A drink for his shoe!

Ao Bing made a tiny cloud.
Drip, drip, drip.
“Gentle rain!” said Wukong.

Each day, they checked the soil.
Dry? A little drink.
Damp? Time to play!

“Red yet?” asked Wukong.
He peeked under every leaf.
Ao Bing found his tickly nose.

Days went by.
Little yellow flowers opened.
“Hello, tiny stars!”

Then came tiny green tomatoes.
“Ready?” asked Wukong.
“Not yet,” said Ao Bing.

Wukong whispered,
“Please turn red.”
The tomatoes said nothing.
Not even a peep.

They watered. They waited.
Green turned to gold.
Gold turned to orange.
“Almost!”

One morning... red!
Bright, shiny, tomato red!
Wukong did a tiny tomato dance.

Twist, lift, plop.
They picked the red ones.
The green ones stayed to grow.

They washed their little harvest.
Red tomatoes. Cool water.
“Now we can taste them!”

They shared sweet tomato bites.
“We grew these,” said Wukong.
Ao Bing smiled. “Together.”

How do I print a little book?

Download the booklet edition. Print on US Letter, landscape, double-sided with short-edge flipping, at actual size / 100%, one PDF page per sheet, in normal page order. Each PDF page already contains two book pages.

This book uses 4 sheets, with 0 blank book pages added at the end. Nest the sheets, fold through the center and staple along the fold.
