Wukong’s Wibble-Wobble Ride

酷酷的自行车骑起来，却有些摇摇晃晃！

故事全文

绘本正文为英文。这里附上文字版，方便在任何屏幕上阅读、搜索和朗读。

“Look at them go!”
Wukong’s tail went BOING!
“I want to ride, too!”

Wukong put on his helmet.
“Ready!”
“Um… it’s backward!”
Wukong giggled.
“Let’s try again!”

He pushed with his feet.
Scoot. Scoot. Scoot.
“Look! A monkey scooter!”

Feet up…
Wibble! Wobble!
PLOP!
“Hello, grass.”

“Maybe the bell will help.”
Ding! Ding!
The bell worked.
The wobble stayed.

Pigsy held the seat.
“Eyes ahead. Nice and slow.”
Rabbit cheered.
“You can do it!”

Wukong tried again.
One push.
One pedal.
Two.

He wobbled left.
His tail wobbled right.
“My tail is learning, too!”

He tried a little every day.
Some rides were short.
Some were wobbly.
He kept trying.

Then, one evening…
Pigsy let go.
Wukong kept going!

“I’m doing it!”
His scarf fluttered.
His tail made a loop.
Even his ears felt proud.

He learned to turn.
He learned to stop.
Just before a puddle.
“After you, little duck.”

Now they rode together.
No rush.
Just happy wheels.

At home, he parked his bike.
“Goodnight, wheels.”
“Goodnight, wobbles.”

Wukong curled up in bed.
In his dreams,
the path was smooth.
And the wheels whispered,
“Shhh…”
